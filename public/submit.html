Draft a notice

Prepare an event, change to opening hours or call-out for review. Choose where you would like it shown, then export the draft or copy a prompt to adapt it for those outlets.

Notice details

Complete the required details and choose at least one public outlet. For paid work, volunteering and C-Hours, see local opportunities. Direct people to the Council disaster dashboard for official emergency information.

Use Queensland local time.

Keep it to 600 characters. Include practical details people can act on and leave out private contact details.

Use the publisher’s website, official update or public event page. For bus service changes, check the operator’s update for the route, direction, affected stops and effective times.

Where may this notice appear?

Choose one or more public outlets. Standard displays at busy stops use the same layout as venue TVs and onboard bus screens. Choose touchscreen kiosks where people can tap for details. The reviewed feed is public.

Review and export

The editor keeps your draft in this browser. Download the Markdown and share it with an authorised local reviewer. For a correction, renewal or removal, give the reviewer the original notice’s ID or public source and say which outlets should change.

To discuss a first island notice or find the right reviewer, contact Luke through Strange But True.
