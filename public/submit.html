Draft a notice

Tell people what’s happening, where to go and who can confirm it. Choose where the notice may appear, then download a copy for review. The draft stays in this browser until you share it.

Notice details

Write your own notice or use the workshop’s film night example for practice. A reviewer needs a public source, a take-down time and permission for each outlet.

Use Queensland local time.

Keep it to 600 characters. Include practical details people can act on and leave out private contact details.

Link to the page a reader can check, such as the organiser’s event listing, the employer’s vacancy or the operator’s service update.

Where may this notice appear?

Choose one or more public outlets. Standard displays at busy stops use the same layout as venue TVs and onboard bus screens. Choose touchscreen kiosks where people can tap for details. The reviewed feed is public.

Review and export

Send the downloaded text file to the person responsible for checking your notice. If an existing notice needs changing or removing, include its public link or ID and say which displays need updating.

To discuss a notice for the proposed network, contact Luke through Strange But True.
