Grants & funding

Fund a lasting island network.

The Straddie Noticeboard Network needs funding to install community displays across Minjerribah and keep people, equipment and information supported year after year.

The December–January school-holiday period is a proposed launch window. Opening dates and locations would be agreed with participating hosts and the operating team.

Build a long-term budget

What funding would support

  • Displays across the island
    A phone-friendly board, screens at venues, on buses and ferries, and displays or battery-backed kiosks at agreed busy stops.
  • Local media training and work
    Supervised reporting, captioning and publishing, with paid operating roles budgeted.
  • Reliable everyday service
    Named people to check and correct notices, maintain devices and rehearse outages after launch.

The legal applicant, venue agreements, staffing and funding are yet to be confirmed.

Build the funding case

  1. Name the applicant and operator

    Confirm the legal applicant, authorised signatory, asset owner and long-term operator. If an eligible organisation agrees to administer funding, record the auspice in writing. Set out Ready SETCO’s proposed convening and moderation role, decision-making, complaints, financial reporting and who covers paid shifts and technical faults.

  2. Document demand and site agreements

    Ask people in Dunwich, Amity Point and Point Lookout where they wait and which notices they miss. Seek agreements from clubs, shops, ferry and bus operators, and the owners of stop infrastructure. For shortlisted stops, record passenger use, accessible boarding space, site and cultural permissions, power, connectivity and who will maintain the display.

  3. Show how the service will work

    Provide a device plan, permission-cleared sample notices, a source and correction workflow, and an accessible media training programme. Show how people could reach official Council, weather and transport information, with issuer permission where an update would be relayed. Include offline, battery and outage testing for selected sites.

  4. Prove it can stay open

    Collect itemised installation, insurance and operating quotes. Document the maintenance schedule, warranties, fault response, replacement reserve and likely funding after the grant. Keep signed commitments, named responsibilities, cash and in-kind contributions, and milestones the applicant can report against.

Keep a quote and funding register

Record quantities, site, quote date, GST, the amount confirmed and the funding gap. Track donated screens, meeting space or host time separately. Only count contributions once and use the funder’s rules for their value.

Budget for wear and changing costs

Decide who holds and replenishes the renewal reserve. Where a replacement is paid from it, keep the same cost out of other lines. Prepare a year-by-year cashflow for inflation, major replacements and costs that vary by season.

Fund the network and keep it running

Take the whole business case to prospective funders. Show the installation costs, annual service costs and renewal plan separately, with signed host commitments and the part each funding source would cover. Confirm which costs, dates and applicants each funder accepts before spending, and allocate each cost once.

A serious approach for the frontier rack would include site feasibility, grid and cooling capacity, network and security requirements, supplier quotes, proposed community and modelling workloads, legal ownership and committed ongoing support. Show how the island uses the network

Agree on measures with hosts, publishers and funders after scope is confirmed. Record useful totals and collect first-hand feedback with permission.

  • Coverage and access. Record which places and venue types a resident or visitor can use. Ask whether they found, read and understood what they needed.
  • Publishing. Measure turnaround, whether corrections reached each screen and whether expired notices came down on time.
  • Training and work. Record how many participants produce an accessible, source-checked item and who can hand the work over. Record paid work and voluntary help separately.
  • Service and renewal. Record faults, restoration time, tested offline and battery performance, actual costs and whether reserves cover the planned replacements.

Sample project wording

We seek funding to install and sustain a community noticeboard network across Minjerribah. Reviewed notices could reach residents and visitors on phones, in venues, aboard buses and ferries and at busy stops. The budget would cover agreements for using existing screens and installation of battery-backed touchscreens at suitable sites. A proposed school-holiday launch would introduce a year-round service with supervised media training, paid operations, maintenance and a replacement reserve. We would report public use, reliability and the costs of keeping the network available.

Add the confirmed applicant and operator, permissions, costed sites, funding request and evidence of local benefit before using this wording.
