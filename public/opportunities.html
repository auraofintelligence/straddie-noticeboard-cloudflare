Work & volunteering

Find paid roles or ways to volunteer on Minjerribah. If you’ve already given time to a community activity, try a worksheet you can keep or share with the organiser.

Find opportunities Explore C-Hours

Find opportunities

Applications and current roles are on the providers’ websites. A proposed island-wide school-holiday launch could create a need for people to help check notices, support venues and maintain screens. Ready SETCO is the proposed convenor. Agree duties, paid shifts and voluntary roles before inviting people to take them on.

Paid work

Search for 4183, Dunwich, Amity Point or Point Lookout. Check whether a listing is on the island or the mainland. Ask the employer about the duties, pay, roster and who will supervise the work.

Search Workforce Australia
Express interest in paid work through Ready SETCO Check Fair Work guidance on paid and unpaid work

Volunteering

Choose a role you want to take on. Check the location and commitments, ask what training or support is available and contact the organisation directly. Bushcare at Point Lookout is one local program to explore.

Find roles with Volunteering Queensland
Search around Dunwich on SEEK Volunteer Find Point Lookout Bushcare through Council

Community Hours (C-Hours)

The C-Hour proposal would recognise one checked hour of voluntary human contribution as one C-Hour, without a money value. Keep paid hours and wages separate. This worksheet helps you describe a voluntary session and calculate its length for a conversation with the activity organiser.

Work out a voluntary session

The worksheet runs in this browser. Use plain activity descriptions and role names. Leave out people’s names, contacts and private details.

Count time you freely gave during the activity. Keep breaks, paid time and machine time separate.

Private worksheet

Review the activity and duration yourself. If you both agree to share it, ask the organiser or the identified role to confirm the time. The worksheet is an unconfirmed draft and carries no credit.

75 minutes would be 1 hour and 15 minutes.
