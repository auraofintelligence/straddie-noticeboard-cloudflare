Four checks when an update is shared

  1. IssuerWho released the update?
  2. TimeWhen was it issued or changed?
  3. AreaDoes it cover your location or route?
  4. RecheckOpen the original for the latest instructions.

A saved screenshot or a notice on a venue screen may be out of date. If you cannot recheck it, its current status is unknown.

Prepare for lost power or signal

  • Write down important contacts, your address or campsite and your household or travel plan. Keep a copy on paper with a torch, spare batteries and a charged power bank.
  • Keep a battery-powered radio. Council lists ABC Radio Brisbane 612 AM and Bay FM 100.3 FM for local updates.
  • For island households, Council advises keeping five to seven days of essentials such as food, water, medicines and pet supplies.
  • If you are visiting, ask your accommodation or campsite host how they share updates. Confirm ferry changes with your operator before travelling.
