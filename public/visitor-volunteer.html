Visitor and volunteer operations

From Straddie towards Brisbane 2032.

Build the ability to welcome visitors, support volunteers and keep local information accurate on Minjerribah. What works at ordinary island events could give organisers elsewhere in Queensland a tested model to consider.

Choose a role

Volunteer coordinator

Arrange an event lead, a welcome volunteer and cover for breaks. Confirm who has agreed to each role, who can take over and what support they need.

Keep names, contacts and changes to shifts in the private roster. Paid work has its own agreed pay and conditions. Record verified voluntary elapsed time once, with permission, in a separate C-Hour record.

Visitor or service operator

A visitor needs a current meeting point and a way to check step-free access. At Dunwich that can include knowing which bus stop to use after the ferry. The bus, ferry or activity operator confirms its own changes. Find the terminal and onward bus.

Build evidence on the island

Try the process with willing local hosts and supervised learners. Use the results to decide what can be maintained as part of the island network and what might be worth proposing to Brisbane 2032 organisers.

Timely information

Record the minutes from an approved correction to visible confirmation at each venue. Count stale copies missed in the outage drill and record the time to reconcile after reconnection.

A workable arrival

Ask a first-time visitor to find the right stop after the ferry, the workshop entrance and a human contact, including when their phone is flat. Test the same handover if an approved new service later uses the pickup point. Explore future island transport.

Staffing that can last

Track agreed shifts filled, whether relief workers can take over, time spent reviewing and the cost of operating a venue display. Report totals and lessons without publishing individual rosters or C-Hour accounts.

Take a tested local offer to organisers

Bring an anonymised handover example, the evidence, an operating budget and the agreements made with participating hosts. Local events give the network a purpose before and beyond 2032.
